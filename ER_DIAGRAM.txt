RELATIONSHIP SUMMARY
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. USERS (1) ──────────── (N) COURSES
   └─ One admin creates many courses

2. USERS (1) ──────────── (N) ENROLLMENTS
   └─ One student has many enrollments
   └─ One admin approves many enrollments

3. COURSES (1) ──────────── (N) ENROLLMENTS
   └─ One course has many enrollment requests

4. ENROLLMENTS (1) ──────────── (N) GRADES
   └─ One enrollment has many grades

5. USERS (1) ──────────── (1) STUDENT_PROFILES
   └─ One student has one profile (unique relationship)

6. COURSES (1) ──────────── (N) COURSE_MATERIALS
   └─ One course has many materials

7. USERS (1) ──────────── (N) NOTIFICATIONS
   └─ One user receives many notifications
